# Personal Macro Workbook (`PERSONAL.XLSB`)

> [!important]
> **Summary Statement**: `PERSONAL.XLSB` is a special hidden workbook that opens automatically in the background whenever Excel launches, making your custom macros, utility tools, and helpers accessible across every open workbook.

---

## 📍 File Storage Location
- Windows Path: `%AppData%\Microsoft\Excel\XLSTART\PERSONAL.XLSB`
- Best for personal productivity shortcuts (e.g., Table of Contents generator, custom formatting, cleaning raw CSV dumps).
